The Maathruthva difference

Why we sprout our grains

Sprouting is an age-old practice with real, measurable benefits — and it's at the heart of everything we make.

🌱

Better absorption

Sprouting lowers natural phytic acid, so the iron and zinc in every spoonful is actually absorbed — not lost.

🤍

Gentle on tummies

The sprouting process pre-digests complex starches, making our food softer and easier for little tummies.

🍃

Naturally nourishing

Sprouting boosts B-vitamins and even generates vitamin C — nutrition created naturally, never added artificially.

Maathruthva means "motherhood"

We started Maathruthva for the simplest reason — we couldn't find baby food we fully trusted. Most packets were full of added sugar, preservatives, and ingredients we couldn't pronounce.

So we made our own. Real grains, sprouted the traditional way to unlock their nutrition and make them gentle on tiny tummies. Dried fruits for natural sweetness. Nothing artificial, ever.

Every batch is made fresh, in small quantities, with the same care we'd give our own children. Because that's exactly who we make it for.
