Why parents choose it

Built for the biggest growth phase

Somewhere around eight months, everything speeds up. Your baby is crawling, grabbing, babbling, and growing faster than ever — and their food needs to keep up.

Growth Mix is our most protein-dense everyday formula. Sprouted moong dal leads with high-quality plant protein, sprouted ragi brings calcium for growing bones, and oats add gentle, sustained energy. Finely milled cashew and pistachio add healthy fats important for brain development.

Naturally creamy, lightly sweet, and something you can feel good about serving every single day. No added sugar, salt, or preservatives.

Good to know

Common questions

At what age can my baby start this?+

Growth Mix is formulated for babies 8 months and older. Always follow your paediatrician's guidance.

My baby hasn't had nuts before — is this safe?+

This formula contains finely milled cashew and pistachio. Introduce each nut individually before first use and watch for any reaction over 72 hours. Consult your paediatrician before introducing nuts.

Can I alternate this with Daily Millet Mix?+

Yes — that's exactly how they're designed. Alternating Growth Mix and Daily Millet Mix across the week gives complete, varied nutrition.

Is there any added sugar?+

Never. Sweetness comes only from dates. No sugar, jaggery, or salt added.
